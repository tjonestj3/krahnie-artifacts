- **Anthropic shipped Claude Fable 5 / Mythos 5** — Anthropic says the new models can work autonomously longer than prior Claude versions, with stronger software engineering, vision, memory, knowledge-work, and life-sciences capabilities. ([Reuters](https://www.reuters.com/world/eu-regulators-order-meta-allow-rival-ai-chatbots-free-access-whatsapp-2026-06-09/), [AP](https://apnews.com/article/meta-whatsapp-european-union-8b4b48acb23acf5686f141a276bb868d))
- **Agentic coding security risk got sharper** — SecurityWeek reported prompt-injection paths in repo comments affecting Claude Code, Gemini CLI, and GitHub Copilot Agents, reinforcing that coding agents need sandboxing and secret isolation before CI/CD autonomy. ([SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/claude-code-gemini-cli-github-copilot-agents-vulnerable-to-prompt-injection-via-comments/))
- **AI infrastructure expansion keeps going global** — Meta signed its first AI data-center deal in India with Reliance, underscoring that frontier and enterprise AI capacity is increasingly a geography, power, and partnerships game. ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/10/meta-signs-first-ai-data-center-deal-in-india-with-reliance/))

**Worth watching:** The near-term edge is shifting from “which model is best?” to “which teams can safely wire agents into real workflows, data, and platform channels.”
