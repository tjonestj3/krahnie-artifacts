([CSO Online](https://www.csoonline.com/article/4069285/salesforce-ai-agents-set-to-assist-enterprises-with-security-and-compliance.html))

([European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/policies/regulatory-framework-ai), [EU AI Act tracker](https://www.euaiact.com/))
