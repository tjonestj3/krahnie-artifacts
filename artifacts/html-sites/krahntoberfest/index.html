📋 Das Programm · The Lineup

Schedule of Shenanigans

A loose plan for a perfect day. Show up when you can, stay as long as you like. (Times approximate — beer-o-clock is flexible.)

2:00 PM
🚪 Doors & First Pour
Gates open, kegs tapped, welcome stein in hand. Come hungry.
2:30 PM
🥨 Pretzel & Brat Spread
Grill fires up — bratwurst, soft pretzels, kraut, and all the mustard you can handle.
3:30 PM
🎯 Lawn Games Tournament
Cornhole, stein-holding contest, and the legendary Krahn Cup bracket. Glory awaits.
5:00 PM
🎶 Oompah Hour
Polka, sing-alongs, and a chicken dance that nobody asked for but everybody joins.
6:30 PM
🏆 Awards & Prizes
Crowning the Stein Champion and handing out wildly unserious trophies.
8:00 PM
🔥 Bonfire & Last Call
Wind down by the fire with good stories and one more for the road. Prost!
