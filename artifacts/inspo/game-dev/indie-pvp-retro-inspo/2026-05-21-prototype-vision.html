Mechanic of the day: Prism Parry Lane

Controls: Mage A quick bolt, S prism trap, D blink. Fighter J slash, K parry, L shoulder dash.

  • Rules: Mage bolts travel in cyan lanes; fighter parry reflects only if the shield color matches the incoming lane.
  • Win condition: First to 3 stocks or most stocks after 60 seconds.
  • Test: Can spectators read “unsafe spell → parry flash → punish dash” in under one second?

Juice checklist

  • 6-frame hitstop on parry, 3-frame on block.
  • Palette flash: cyan magic, amber steel, magenta danger.
  • Screen-edge warning arrows for off-screen blinks.
  • CRT bass thump when timer drops below 10.
  • Chunky dust puffs on dash-cancel landings.
  • UI bars shake only on real damage, not block chip.
  • Reflected projectile changes silhouette, not just color.
  • Post-round “one reason you lost” stat: whiffed parries, trap damage, corner time.
