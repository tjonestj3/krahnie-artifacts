Mechanic of the day

Spell Parry: “Catch the Comet”
  • Controls: Mage Q tap bolt / hold comet. Fighter J slash, K parry, L dash.
  • Rules: Parry window is 8 frames; success freezes both players for 6 frames, flips projectile ownership, and grants fighter one glowing step-cancel.
  • Win: First to 3 stocks; ring-outs count only after 20 seconds to encourage opening duels.
  • Test: Can a spectator identify owner/color, danger timing, and punish opportunity in under one second?

Juice checklist

  • Projectile palette swaps owner: cyan → pink with a single chunky sparkle burst.
  • Hitstop tiers: 3f graze, 6f parry, 10f stock-ending wall splat.
  • CRT bass “thunk” on block; glassy arpeggio on spell charge; silence dip before lethal hit.
  • UI bars shake only on real damage, not chip; meter pips use distinct silhouettes.
  • After-match card: “Most stylish parry,” “longest air chase,” and one GIF-friendly replay beat.
