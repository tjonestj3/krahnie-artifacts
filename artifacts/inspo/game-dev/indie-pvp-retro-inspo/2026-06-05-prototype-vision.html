Prototype arena sketch

Mechanic of the day: Spark Guard

Controls: Mage J quick bolt, K arc orb, L blink. Fighter J slash, K shield dash, L Spark Guard.

Rules: Spark Guard is a 10-frame parry window. If it catches a spell, fighter stores one glowing charge; next slash becomes a reflect wave. Whiffing guard adds 18 frames recovery.

Win condition: First to 3 ring-outs or health breaks. Cover blocks bolts but not arc orbs.

What to test: Can the mage bait parry without feeling helpless, and can the fighter close distance without invalidating zoning?

Juice checklist

  • 2-frame white/palette flash on successful parry.
  • 6-frame hitstop on charge steal; 10-frame on KO.
  • Distinct spell shapes: bolt=line, orb=circle, curse=zigzag.
  • Mana bar ticks audibly refill like arcade coins.
  • Screen-edge danger arrows before ring-out.
  • Controller rumble: short mage cast, chunky shield catch.
  • Post-match tiny replay GIF moment: “PARRY STEAL!”
