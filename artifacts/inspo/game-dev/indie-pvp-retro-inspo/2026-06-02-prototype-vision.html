FIGHTER STAMINA

Mechanic of the day: Arcane Guardbreak Parry

Controls / rules / win condition

  • Mage A tap = slow orb, hold = charged piercing bolt; Mage B blink with afterimage but drains mana.
  • Fighter A slash; Fighter B 12-frame shield-parry that reflects orbs but loses to charged bolts if held too early.
  • Cover blocks normal orbs, platforms enable jump angles, and every hit adds knockback percent instead of raw HP.
  • Win condition: first to 3 ring-outs, with sudden-death lava floor after 90 seconds.

What to test today

  • Can players read the difference between normal orb, charged bolt, and reflected orb in under 250ms?
  • Does fighter feel brave—not helpless—while crossing a mage zoning lane?
  • Do parry successes create clip-worthy hitstop, palette flash, and screen thump without hiding the next input?

Juice checklist

  • 8-frame mage cast anticipation: staff glow → eye flash → orb spawn.
  • Fighter parry ring expands in team color, then snaps inward on success.
  • Projectile trails use distinct silhouettes: orb round, bolt spear, reflected zig-zag.
  • 2-frame global hitstop + 6-frame local freeze on parry; avoid freezing UI bars.
  • CRT bloom only on VFX, not player bodies, to preserve readability.
  • Audio callouts: low “woom” charge, glass “plink” parry, crunchy ring-out bass.
  • Round-end postcard: “Fighter crossed the spell lane!” for social sharing.
  • Wishlist/demo CTA mock: one-button rematch, Discord link, tournament night teaser.
