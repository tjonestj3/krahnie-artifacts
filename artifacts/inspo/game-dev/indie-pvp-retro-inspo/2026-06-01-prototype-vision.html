Mechanic of the day: Prism Parry vs Arc Bolt

Controls

Mage: Aims + FireMage: BlinkFighter: DashFighter: Parry

Mage casts slow, fat Arc Bolts that ricochet once. Fighter has a 10-frame Prism Parry that reflects only the bright core, not the danger trail.

Rules / win / test

Win: first to 3 knockouts or 90-second HP lead. Rule: reflected bolt becomes faster but thinner; mage can blink-catch it with perfect timing. Test: can spectators identify parry success in one frame from palette flash + hitstop?

Juice checklist

  • White silhouette flash on every parry, then cyan/pink afterimage trails.
  • 2-frame freeze on blocked spell, 6-frame freeze on reflected spell.
  • Projectile core brighter than aura; trail is decorative, not damage.
  • Fighter dash dust uses square pixels that point toward intent.
  • Mage cooldown UI floats above staff as three tiny rune pips.
  • Low-health music adds crunchy arpeggio, not volume clutter.
  • Cover blocks bolts but shakes and sheds glowing tiles.
  • End round with instant rematch prompt and shareable duel stat: “3 reflects, 1 blink-catch.”
