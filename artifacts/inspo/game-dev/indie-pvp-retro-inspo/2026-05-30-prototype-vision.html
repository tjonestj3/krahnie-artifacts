Prototype arena sketch

MAGE · mana 3/5 · blink ready
FIGHTER · guard hot · dash spent

Mechanic of the day: Spell Break Clash

Controls

Mage: Move, Jump, Cast Orb, Blink. Fighter: Move, Jump, Dash Slash, Guard/Parry. Both have one shared meter pip for EX cancel.

Rules + win

Best of 5 short rounds. Mage wins by zoning and tagging with charged orbs; Fighter wins by closing space and cashing parries into wall-bounce combos. First to 3 ring-outs or KOs wins.

What to test

Can the fighter parry a projectile on frames 3-8, converting it into a slow return spark, while the mage can feint-cancel charge to bait the parry?

Success signal

Both players should say “I knew what happened” after every hit: colored anticipation frames, screen shake only on confirms, and no VFX hiding hurtboxes.

Juice checklist

  • 1-frame white impact flash on parry; 6-frame hitstop on heavy slash.
  • Mage orb uses cyan core + magenta rim so ownership is readable.
  • Fighter dash leaves orange afterimages that fade before active frames.
  • Mana pips pulse only when actionable, not constantly.
  • Low-pass “CRT thump” on round start; pitched chime for perfect parry.
  • Dust pixels on landing, but keep floor silhouettes clean.
  • Tiny victory taunt sprites for social clips and Discord GIFs.
  • End-round card: “Parries landed / spells baited / closest call.”
