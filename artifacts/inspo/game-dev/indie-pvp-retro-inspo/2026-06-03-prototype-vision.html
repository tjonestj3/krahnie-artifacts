Mechanic of the day: Spellcatch Parry

Controls: Mage A blink, S bolt, D ward. Fighter J dash, K slash, L parry.

Rules: Fighter parry has 9 active frames. Perfect parry catches one bolt, stores it in the blade for 2 seconds, then the next slash releases a short-range arcane wave. Mage can feint-cast to bait early parries.

Win condition: First to 3 stocks, 45-second rounds; timeout awards the lead to space-control score: center time + successful approaches.

What to test today
  • Does the fighter feel brave but not invincible crossing projectile space?
  • Can spectators read “stored spell” in one glance?
  • Is mage feinting fun, or does it slow the duel too much?

Juice checklist

  • 6-frame hitstop on parry, 3-frame on normal hit.
  • Mage bolt palette: cyan core, white rim, magenta afterimage.
  • Fighter stored-spell sword glow visible in HUD portrait.
  • CRT screen shake only on confirmed hits, never on whiffs.
  • “TING!” parry sound pitched by timing quality.
  • Dust puffs on dash start/end to clarify approach windows.
  • Cover cracks when hit by spell, resets each round.
  • Post-round micro-replay: final 2 seconds as a shareable clip.
