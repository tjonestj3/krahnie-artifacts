Mechanic of the day — Guard-Cut Spell Parry

Controls: Mage: A blink, B bolt, hold B slow orb. Fighter: A dash, B slash, tap ↓+B parry.

Rules: Parry reflects fast bolts but only “cracks” slow orbs into two smaller hazards. Fighter gains guard meter on perfect parry; mage regains mana if an orb forces movement without hitting.

Win condition: First to 3 stocks or 60-second timeout decided by arena control gems.

What to test: Can the fighter advance without feeling helpless? Can spectators read bolt vs orb vs reflected shard in one glance?

Juice checklist

  • 6-frame hitstop on parry; 10-frame on KO
  • Palette flash: cyan spell, gold parry, magenta danger
  • Screen-edge warning arrows for offscreen projectiles
  • Dust puffs on dash start and landing
  • Low-health CRT vignette pulse
  • Distinct spell cast syllable per projectile size
  • Controller rumble: tiny tick on block, heavy thump on reflect
  • End-round instant rematch countdown: 3…2…1…zap
