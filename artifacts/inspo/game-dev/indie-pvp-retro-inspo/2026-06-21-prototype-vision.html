Mechanic of the day: Clash Window

Mage: Aim + SpellFighter: Dash + GuardBoth: Jump / Drop

  • Rules: Fighter guard creates a 10-frame parry cone. Mage projectiles have colored cores: blue bolts can be parried, pink hexes pierce guard but lose speed through cover.
  • Win condition: Best-of-5 rounds, 60 seconds, ring-outs deal bonus stun instead of instant death.
  • Test: Can both players understand why a projectile bounced, shattered, or pierced within one replay glance?

Juice checklist

  • 2-frame white flash on parry + 6-frame hitstop only for the two involved actors.
  • Projectile trails use shape language: circles = parryable, diamonds = armor-piercing, squares = terrain-affecting.
  • CRT bloom reserved for super meter and lethal hits, not every particle.
  • Low-health music layer: mage arpeggio vs fighter drum fill.
  • Round-end freeze-frame with comic-book labels: “COUNTER-SPELL!”, “IRON CLASH!”
