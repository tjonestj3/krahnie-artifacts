Mechanic of the day: Spell-Parry Ping Pong

Controls: Mage: A/D move, J bolt, K teleport. Fighter: ←/→ move, 1 slash, 2 parry dash.

Rules: Mage bolt is slow but curves around cover. Fighter parry has 8 active frames; perfect parry reflects bolt as a golden shockwave and grants 12 frames of armor.

Win: Best-of-5 stocks or first to 3 crystal breaks.

Test: Can new players understand “blue danger / gold reflection” in one round without text?

Juice checklist

  • Palette flash: cyan cast, white hitstop, gold reflect.
  • 3-frame anticipation pose before every dangerous spell.
  • Chunky parry clang + 6px screen kick, never enough to hide hitboxes.
  • Mana/rage bars pulse only when a comeback option is available.
  • Impact freeze: 4f light hit, 8f parry, 12f stock break.
  • After-match share card: “I reflected 3 bolts in 2.1s.”
