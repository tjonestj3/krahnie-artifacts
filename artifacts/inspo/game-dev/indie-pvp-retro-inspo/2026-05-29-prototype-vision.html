Mechanic of the day: Wager Parry

Mage: A/D moveW blinkJ boltK ward-parryFighter: arrowsN dashM slash, guard-parry

Rules: parry costs one shared momentum pip. If timed inside a 10-frame window, projectile or slash rebounds as a slow glowing rune; if mistimed, the player suffers guard heat and brief vulnerability. Win: best of 5 stocks or first to bank 5 rebound runes. Test: does meter wagering create hype decisions without hiding who is winning?

Juice checklist

  • Palette flash: cyan for magic, amber for steel, pink for danger.
  • Big readable silhouettes; no same-hue projectile/background overlap.
  • Guard heat UI pulses before break.
  • Low-bit crowd sting when meter is wagered.
  • Screen shake only on confirmed hits, not whiffs.
  • Afterimage trail for fighter dash; rune motes for mage blink.
