Mechanic of the day: Spell-Parry Clash

Controls
Mage: A boltMage: B rune trapFighter: X slashFighter: Y parry dash
Rules
  • Parry dash has 10 active frames, 22 recovery frames.
  • Perfect parry reflects bolt as a slow gold orb; late parry blocks but loses stamina.
  • Mage rune trap arms after 0.4s, forcing fighter to choose jump, wait, or commit.
Win condition & test
  • First to 3 stocks or most stocks after 90 seconds.
  • Test whether the fighter can cross screen without feeling helpless.
  • Watch for VFX clutter: every projectile needs unique color, shape, and sound.

Juice checklist

  • 2-frame palette flash on parry success; 6-frame hitstop on reflected projectile hit.
  • CRT bloom only on silhouettes/projectiles, never on platform collision edges.
  • Distinct audio: mage cast = rising chime, fighter parry = metal clap, reflect = coin burst.
  • Afterimage trail for fighter dash; square-particle embers for mage runes.
  • Big round-start callout: “ZONE” over mage, “BREAK” over fighter.
  • Instant rematch button and post-round stat: “Parries landed / traps triggered.”
